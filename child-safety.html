# Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) Standards **Last Updated:** June 15, 2026 ## Our Commitment At Wino Voice Chat, we are committed to protecting children and preventing any form of child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). We maintain a zero-tolerance policy toward content, behavior, or activities that exploit, endanger, or sexually abuse minors. Our platform is designed to foster a safe and respectful community, and we actively work to detect, prevent, and remove harmful content. ## Prohibited Content and Behavior The following are strictly prohibited on our platform: * Any content depicting, promoting, or facilitating child sexual abuse or exploitation. * Sexualized images, videos, audio, or text involving minors. * Grooming, solicitation, or attempts to establish inappropriate relationships with minors. * Sharing, requesting, storing, or distributing child sexual abuse material (CSAM). * Human trafficking or exploitation of minors. * Encouraging minors to engage in sexual activities. * Any attempt to evade our safety systems or moderation policies. Users who engage in such activities will be permanently banned, and their accounts may be reported to relevant authorities. ## Reporting CSAE Content Users can report content, messages, rooms, or user accounts that they believe violate these standards. Reports are reviewed as quickly as possible by our moderation systems and administrators. We prioritize reports involving the safety of minors and take immediate action when necessary. ## Content Moderation and Enforcement We employ a combination of: * Automated detection systems. * User reporting tools. * Manual moderation and review. * Account restrictions and permanent bans. When CSAE-related content or behavior is detected, we may: 1. Remove the offending content immediately. 2. Suspend or permanently terminate the violating account. 3. Preserve relevant evidence as required by law. 4. Report confirmed cases to the appropriate legal authorities or organizations. ## Cooperation With Law Enforcement We cooperate with law enforcement agencies and authorized child protection organizations when legally required or when necessary to protect children from abuse and exploitation. We may provide relevant information in accordance with applicable laws and regulations. ## Age Restrictions Our services are not intended for children below the minimum age permitted by applicable laws or platform requirements. Users who misrepresent their age or attempt to use the platform in violation of our age requirements may have their accounts restricted or permanently removed. ## User Safety We encourage users to: * Report suspicious or harmful behavior immediately. * Avoid sharing personal information with strangers. * Block and report users who engage in inappropriate conduct. * Contact local authorities if they believe a child is in immediate danger. ## Contact Information If you have questions regarding these CSAE Standards or wish to report concerns directly, please contact us: Email: [codecafe.eg@gmail.com](mailto:codecafe.eg@gmail.com) We take all reports seriously and are committed to maintaining a safe environment for our community. ## Policy Updates We may update these CSAE Standards periodically to reflect changes in legal requirements, industry best practices, or our services. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
