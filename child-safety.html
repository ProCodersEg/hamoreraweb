Child Safety Standards

Last Updated: June 15, 2026

Our Commitment

At Wino Voice Chat, we are committed to protecting children and preventing any form of child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE).

We maintain a zero-tolerance policy toward content, behavior, or activities that exploit, endanger, or sexually abuse minors.

Our platform is designed to foster a safe and respectful community, and we actively work to detect, prevent, and remove harmful content.

Prohibited Content and Behavior

The following are strictly prohibited:

  • Any content depicting, promoting, or facilitating child sexual abuse or exploitation.
  • Sexualized images, videos, audio, or text involving minors.
  • Grooming, solicitation, or attempts to establish inappropriate relationships with minors.
  • Sharing, requesting, storing, or distributing child sexual abuse material (CSAM).
  • Human trafficking or exploitation of minors.
  • Encouraging minors to engage in sexual activities.
  • Any attempt to evade our safety systems or moderation policies.

Users who engage in such activities will be permanently banned and may be reported to relevant authorities.

Reporting CSAE Content

Users can report content, messages, rooms, or user accounts that they believe violate these standards.

Reports are reviewed as quickly as possible. We prioritize reports involving the safety of minors and take immediate action when necessary.

Content Moderation and Enforcement

We employ a combination of:

  • Automated detection systems.
  • User reporting tools.
  • Manual moderation and review.
  • Account restrictions and permanent bans.

When CSAE-related content or behavior is detected, we may:

  1. Remove the offending content immediately.
  2. Suspend or permanently terminate the violating account.
  3. Preserve relevant evidence as required by law.
  4. Report confirmed cases to the appropriate legal authorities or organizations.

Cooperation With Law Enforcement

We cooperate with law enforcement agencies and authorized child protection organizations when legally required or when necessary to protect children from abuse and exploitation.

We may provide relevant information in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

Age Restrictions

Our services are not intended for children below the minimum age permitted by applicable laws or platform requirements.

Users who misrepresent their age or attempt to use the platform in violation of our age requirements may have their accounts restricted or permanently removed.

User Safety

  • Report suspicious or harmful behavior immediately.
  • Avoid sharing personal information with strangers.
  • Block and report users who engage in inappropriate conduct.
  • Contact local authorities if you believe a child is in immediate danger.

Policy Updates

We may update these Child Safety Standards periodically to reflect changes in legal requirements, industry best practices, or our services.

Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
